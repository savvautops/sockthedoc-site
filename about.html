Hi, I'm Savv

I'm a web developer from Winnipeg. I build things on the internet for a living, and somewhere along the way I got tired of health advice that assumed I had a personal chef, a home gym, and eight free hours a day.

So I started reading the actual research and writing down what I learned in plain language. That turned into a Pinterest account, and the Pinterest account turned into this site.

I'm not a doctor, a dietitian, or a guru. I'm a regular person who likes figuring out what actually works. Everything here is informational, and the disclaimers are real: talk to your doctor about your body, not a website.

Why a duck?

Sock started as a doodle for the Pinterest account, a duck in a stethoscope for a page called SockTheDoc. People liked him, so he stayed. He has no medical opinions and offers no medical advice. He handles morale.

What I believe

  • Small beats heroic. Habits that survive your worst week beat plans that only work on your most motivated Monday.
  • Plain language. If it needs a medical dictionary, it gets rewritten until it doesn't.
  • Honest about limits. I'll tell you what worked for real people and what the research suggests, and I'll say clearly when something is just my opinion.
  • No shame, ever. Your body, your pace. This site helps, it doesn't scold.

What you'll never find here

Miracle cures, detox teas, "one weird trick" promises, or before-and-after transformations. If something sounds too good to be true, I'll be the first to say so.

A note from Sock: Sock is a duck, not a doctor. Nothing on this site is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The articles share general wellness information for educational purposes only. Always consult a qualified health professional about any medical condition, and before making changes to your diet, exercise, sleep, or medications. If something here ever conflicts with your doctor's guidance, your doctor wins, every time.

How the site stays free

SockTheDoc is free to read. Right now it runs on its own: no advertisers, no affiliate links. If we join an affiliate program or take on sponsors in the future, we’ll say so here and in the affiliate disclosure.
