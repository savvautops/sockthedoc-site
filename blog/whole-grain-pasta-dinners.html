5 Whole Grain Pasta Dinners

S

By Savv
November 2026 · 4 min read

Whole grain pasta keeps the bran and germ of the wheat, so it brings more fibre and a nuttier, heartier bite than regular pasta. It also stands up best to bold, chunky sauces, which is convenient, because those are exactly the sauces that load a pasta night with vegetables and legumes. Five dinners, one box of pasta each.

Two tips before the sauces

Whole grain pasta rewards attention: salt the water well, and pull the pasta a minute before the box says, because it keeps cooking in the sauce and can go from firm to floppy quickly. Save a mug of the starchy pasta water before draining; a splash loosens any sauce and helps it cling. Now, the dinners.

  1. A veggie packed marinara bowl. Simmer a simple tomato marinara and load it with zucchini, peppers, mushrooms, and spinach, then toss with penne or fusilli, the shapes that trap chunky sauce. The vegetables bulk up the bowl, so a modest portion of pasta still eats like a full dinner. A handful of basil or a spoon of pesto on top finishes it.
  2. A lemon and herb pasta. The lightest plate here. Toss hot spaghetti with olive oil, lemon zest and juice, garlic, parsley, and a pinch of chilli flakes. The bright, sharp sauce is a perfect match for whole grain pasta's nuttiness, and the whole thing takes as long as the pasta takes to boil. Add peas or asparagus tips if the crisper offers them.
  3. A creamy mushroom pasta. Brown a pile of sliced mushrooms in olive oil until they give up their water and turn golden, add garlic, then stir in a splash of cream or a spoon of Greek yogurt off the heat. Toss with pasta and plenty of black pepper. Mushrooms bring the deep, savoury flavour that makes a meatless dinner feel complete.
  4. A tomato and white bean pasta. Simmer canned white beans in tomato sauce with garlic and rosemary until the beans go creamy at the edges, then toss with short pasta. The beans add fibre and protein and half of them break down into the sauce, thickening it for free. This is a pantry dinner in the best sense: everything in it keeps for months.
  5. A pesto pasta with greens. Stir basil pesto through hot pasta with a splash of pasta water, then fold in handfuls of spinach or chopped kale and let the heat wilt them. Add green beans or broccoli if you want more crunch. Pesto's strong flavour is exactly what whole grain pasta wants, and dinner lands in about 15 minutes.
