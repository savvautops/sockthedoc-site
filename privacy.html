Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SockTheDoc (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects when you visit health.nano-nodes.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language because privacy policies shouldn’t require a law degree, or a duck translator.

1. Who we are (data controller)

SockTheDoc is operated by Savv Studio. For privacy questions, contact us at sockonconcrete@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a) Information you give us directly. When you sign up for our email list or download a free guide, we collect your email address and any name you choose to provide. When you contact us by email, we receive your message and email address.

b) Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we automatically collect basic technical information: pages viewed, time spent, referring site, browser type, device type, and approximate location (derived from IP address). Your IP address may be logged by our hosting provider for security purposes.

c) Purchases. If you buy a paid guide from us in the future, payment is processed by our payment provider (e.g., Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy). We do not see or store your full payment card details; the provider shares only what we need to fulfill your order (such as your email address and purchase record).

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and the Site is not directed at children.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you the free guides you request and the email newsletter you signed up for.
  • To respond to your questions and messages.
  • To understand which content is useful, so we can make more of it (via aggregated analytics).
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We never sell your personal information to anyone. We don’t rent our email list, trade it, or share it with data brokers.

4. Email list

Our email list is opt-in: you join by submitting a signup form on the Site. We use a third-party email service provider (such as ConvertKit, Buttondown, or Mailchimp) to deliver emails; that provider processes your email address on our behalf under its own privacy policy and our agreement with it.

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also unsubscribe anytime by emailing sockonconcrete@gmail.com with the subject “Unsubscribe.” We will remove you promptly.

5. Cookies and analytics

The Site may use a privacy-respecting analytics tool to understand aggregate traffic patterns (for example, which articles are popular). This analytics data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

We may use strictly necessary cookies or local storage for basic site functionality (such as remembering that you dismissed a notice). We do not use advertising trackers or cross-site tracking cookies.

You can block cookies through your browser settings; the Site will still work, though some preferences may not be remembered.

6. Affiliate links

Some links on the Site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the merchant may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. When you click an affiliate link, the merchant’s site may set its own cookies to track the referral. Those cookies are governed by the merchant’s privacy policy, not ours. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

7. How we share information

We share personal information only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers: email delivery, analytics, and hosting providers who process data on our behalf and are bound to protect it.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.
  • Business transfer: if the Site is ever sold or transferred, your information may transfer as part of the business assets, and we will notify you.

8. Data retention

We keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed, and remove it when you unsubscribe (aside from a suppression record so we don’t email you again by accident). Analytics data is retained in aggregated form. Contact emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email sockonconcrete@gmail.com. We read every message and respond within a reasonable time.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including serving the Site over HTTPS and limiting access to subscriber data. No system is perfectly secure, but we don’t cut corners here.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this Privacy Policy in a material way, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify subscribers by email. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email sockonconcrete@gmail.com. We read everything.
