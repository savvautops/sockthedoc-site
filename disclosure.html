Affiliate Disclosure

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Short version: SockTheDoc has no active affiliate programs right now. No link on this site currently earns us a commission. If that ever changes, we’ll list every program below and clearly mark the affiliate links.

What’s an affiliate link?

If we join an affiliate program in the future, some links to products we recommend may be affiliate links. An affiliate link works exactly like a normal link for you, but it tells the merchant we sent you. If you make a purchase, the merchant pays us a small percentage as a thank-you. The price you pay is identical either way.

Where you’ll see them

If we join an affiliate program, affiliate links may appear in blog articles, guides, resource pages, and emails. Where practical, we’ll note it near the link (for example: “This is an affiliate link, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.”). This page serves as our general disclosure, in line with FTC endorsement guidelines: any material connection between us and a merchant is disclosed clearly and conspicuously.

Our promise about recommendations

  • We only recommend products we’d genuinely suggest to a friend, commission or not.
  • A commission never changes what we say about a product. If something’s mediocre, we’ll say so or skip it.
  • We don’t let merchants pay for placement in our guides or reviews.

Programs we use

Right now SockTheDoc has no active affiliate program memberships, so there is nothing to name here. When that changes, for example if we join Amazon Associates, we will list each program on this page and keep it current. The promise above applies regardless: we recommend things we would suggest to a friend, commission or not.
