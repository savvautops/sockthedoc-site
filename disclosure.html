Affiliate Disclosure

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Short version: some links on SockTheDoc are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. That’s how the duck keeps the pond running and the guides free.

What’s an affiliate link?

When we recommend a product — a sleep aid, a kitchen gadget, a book, a wellness program — the link may be an affiliate link. It works exactly like a normal link for you, but it tells the merchant we sent you. If you make a purchase, the merchant pays us a small percentage as a thank-you. The price you pay is identical either way.

Where you’ll see them

Affiliate links may appear in blog articles, guides, resource pages, and emails. Where practical, we’ll note it near the link (for example: “This is an affiliate link — we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.”). This page serves as our general disclosure, in line with FTC endorsement guidelines: any material connection between us and a merchant is disclosed clearly and conspicuously.

Our promise about recommendations

  • We only recommend products we’d genuinely suggest to a friend — commission or not.
  • A commission never changes what we say about a product. If something’s mediocre, we’ll say so or skip it.
  • We don’t let merchants pay for placement in our guides or reviews.

Amazon and other programs

SockTheDoc may participate in affiliate programs including (but not limited to) Amazon Associates, ClickBank, and other affiliate networks. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. Each program has its own terms; commissions vary by merchant.
